    Camera system¶

    The camera is built on Unreal's Gameplay Cameras. A Blueprint camera director picks camera rigs every frame from the character's state, and the component AC_CameraSystem handles the perspective, attaches the camera to the right mesh, and draws the crosshair.
    Folder /GASPALS/Systems/CameraSystem
    Component AC_CameraSystem (on both pawns)
    Camera asset CameraAsset_SandboxCharacter (the pawns' Gameplay Camera component uses it)
    Director CameraDirector_SandboxCharacter (Blueprint camera director)
    Rigs Rigs/ (chooser tables CHT_CameraRig* and camera rigs)
    Widget W_Crosshair
    Camera shake CameraShake_Basic (played by the takedown montages' camera shake notify)
    Input Perspective 1, style 2, shoulder Alt / ++r3++
    Settings Camera reference

    Perspectives¶

    Perspective Camera Attached to
    ThirdPerson (default) Over-the-shoulder camera with collision push and damping. The visible mesh's Third Person Camera Bone (root)
    TopDown Fixed-angle camera above the character; the character aims at the mouse cursor or with the right stick. The visible mesh's Third Person Camera Bone (root)
    SideScroller Fixed side view; aims like top-down. The visible mesh's root
    FirstPerson Eye camera on the first-person mesh. See First person. The first-person lead mesh's First Person Camera Bone (VB Camera virtual bone, set per character on AC_FirstPersonRendering)

    1 cycles through Allowed Perspectives in their order. By default: third person, top-down, side-scroller, first person, back to third person. The visible mesh is the retargeted character's when one is active, so the camera follows it.

    Choosing the perspectives¶

    Allowed Perspectives lists the views players can use. Common setups:

    Game Allowed Perspectives Result
    First person only FirstPerson Starts in first person; 1 does nothing.
    Third person only ThirdPerson No first-person meshes are built; 1 does nothing. You can also delete AC_FirstPersonRendering from the pawn and the characters.
    Third and first person ThirdPerson, FirstPerson 1 toggles between them.

    If Camera Perspective (the start view) is not in the list, the camera starts in the first allowed one and shows an on-screen warning. FirstPerson only counts as available when the pawn has AC_FirstPersonRendering. With only first person allowed, cover's First Person Sticky Cover setting SwitchToThirdPerson acts like Disabled (Cover); a Camera Perspective During Ragdoll that is not allowed keeps the current view.

    Styles¶

    The style picks a variant per perspective. 2 cycles it; DDCvar.CameraStyle sets it too.

    Style Third person Top-down / side-scroller
    Style_1 Close CameraRig_TD_1 / CameraRig_SS_1
    Style_2 (default) Medium _2
    Style_3 Far _3
    Style_4 Debug view (CameraRig_DebugView) _4

    How the rig is chosen¶

    Every frame the director:

    1. Reads the pawn's Get_PropertiesForCamera: perspective, style, mode (Free, Strafe or Aim, from the pawn's rotation mode and aim input), shoulder side and stance.
    2. Evaluates the chooser table CHT_CameraRig, which branches by perspective into CHT_CameraRigs_ThirdPerson, _FirstPerson, _TopDown or _SideScroller. Third person picks by style and mode: nine rigs CameraRig_TP_<Close|Medium|Far>_<Freecam|Strafe|Aim>; the Aim rigs blend in smoothly. The fallback is CameraRig_TP_Medium_Freecam.
    3. Activates the result, and the First Person Scale Rig in first person when the character uses the Split first-person mode.
    4. In third person (except the debug style), stacks the offset rigs from Camera Offset Rigs on AC_CameraSystem:
    Entry Default rig When Offset
    Crouch Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_Crouch Crouched 50 cm down
    Right Shoulder Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_Right Right shoulder (or a cover edge on the right) 100 cm side
    Cover Low Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_CoverLow Behind low cover 80 cm up
    Cover Edge Right Rig / Cover Edge Left Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_CoverEdgeRight / Left At a cover wall end 60 cm to the open side
    First Person Scale Rig CameraRig_FP_Scale First person, Split mode First-person scale 0.25 (keeps arms and weapon out of walls)
    In Air Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_InAir Jumping or falling 30 cm back, 20 cm up
    Sliding Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_Sliding Sliding (Mover pawn) 30 cm down
    Traversing Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_Traversing Vaulting or climbing 40 cm back
    Ragdoll Rig CameraRig_TP_Offset_Ragdoll Ragdolling in third person 60 cm back, 40 cm up

    Each pawn sets its own rigs on its camera component, so two characters can use different offsets. Point an entry at your own rig to change an offset, or clear it to turn that offset off. The director's own Camera Offset Rigs is only used for a pawn without AC_CameraSystem.

    The chooser table itself is fixed in the director (the engine's Evaluate Chooser node takes the table as a node setting, not an input). To change which main rig is used when, edit CHT_CameraRig and its nested tables, or duplicate them and the director.

    First person needs no crouch offset: the first-person camera follows the head bone, so it lowers with the head when the character crouches (about 26 cm, measured on the Mover pawn with the UEFN mannequin).

    The persistent layers CameraRig_Layer_Global and CameraRig_Layer_Base are activated when the director starts.

    The base rigs hold the shared parameters, and the variants override them:

    Base rig Parameters
    ThirdPerson/CameraRig_TP_Base Boom arm offset, camera offset, field of view, forward / lateral / vertical damping, collision push
    TopDown/CameraRig_TopDown_Base, SideScroller/CameraRig_SideScroller_Base Camera translation and rotation, field of view, damping
    FirstPerson/CameraRig_FP_Base Field of view, offset, a First Person node (keeps first-person meshes out of the third-person shadow; do not remove it)

    To change the look, open the rigs in the Gameplay Cameras editor. To change which rig is used when, edit the chooser tables. See Customize the camera.

    Crosshair and aim¶

    For the locally controlled player, AC_CameraSystem creates the Crosshair Widget Class (default W_Crosshair) and keeps it centered on the aim point:

    • Third and first person: a line trace (Aim Trace Channel, default Visibility; Aim Trace Distance, default 100 m) from the camera along its forward vector; the dot sits where it hits.
    • Top-down and side-scroller: the world point under the mouse cursor (Fixed Camera Mouse Aim Type), or with a gamepad, 3 m out along the right stick direction.

    GetCrosshairAim returns the aim origin, direction, point, whether it hit, and a flattened direction. The pawns pass it on through the pawn interface function Get_AimRay (with the trace channel and distance), which is what cover targeting and aim from cover read; aim from cover traces with the same channel and length, so the crosshair and the cover line of fire never disagree.

    The camera component only creates and places the crosshair, at the point the aim ray hits. The widget decides how it looks: W_Crosshair is a dot in its Default Color (white). Cover targets are shown by the cover marker, not the crosshair; a custom crosshair can still ask the cover system (IsCoverTargeted).

    Turn Show Crosshair off to hide the dot (it is collapsed, not destroyed). The aim ray, cover targeting and the cover marker keep working.

    Ragdoll camera¶

    With Change Perspective During Ragdoll on (default), the camera switches to Camera Perspective During Ragdoll (top-down) while the CMC pawn ragdolls (AC_RagdollSystem), and back afterwards. It only switches when that perspective is in Allowed Perspectives. Allow Camera Control During Ragdoll decides whether the player can still look around. The Mover pawn uses its own ragdoll (a movement mode) and keeps the camera.

    Settings¶

    On AC_CameraSystem:

    Setting Default Use
    Camera Perspective ThirdPerson Start perspective. Must be in Allowed Perspectives.
    Allowed Perspectives ThirdPerson, TopDown, SideScroller, FirstPerson Views the player can use; 1 cycles them in this order.
    Third Person Camera Bone root Bone or socket the third-person and top-down cameras attach to.
    Camera Style Medium (Style_2) Start style.
    Is Camera Right Shoulder false Start shoulder.
    Show Crosshair true Show the crosshair dot for the local player.
    Crosshair Widget Class W_Crosshair Any User Widget; it is added to the viewport and centered on the aim point.
    Camera Offset Rigs the ten rigs above Offset rigs the director stacks on the main rig. Clear an entry to turn that offset off.
    Pass Movement Mode To Camera true The pawn tells the camera whether it is on the ground, in the air, sliding, traversing or ragdolling, for the movement mode rigs (and for your own chooser rows). Off = always on the ground. The CMC pawn has no slide.
    Aim Trace Channel Visibility Channel of the aim ray (crosshair, aim from cover).
    Aim Trace Distance 10000 Length of the aim ray in cm.
    Change Perspective During Ragdoll true See above.
    Camera Perspective During Ragdoll TopDown See above.
    Allow Camera Control During Ragdoll false See above.
    Fixed Camera Mouse Aim Type MouseTraceWithPositionFallback Fixed cameras: aim at the world point under the cursor, or use the cursor's screen position.

    DDCVar.NewGameplayCameraSystem.Enable (project setting, default on) must stay on. Off, the pawns fall back to a plain spring arm camera and none of this runs.

    Using the camera from Blueprints¶

    Call Use
    SetCameraPerspective(NewPerspective) Change the perspective (re-attaches the camera, tells the server, fires OnCameraPerspectiveChanged).
    OnCameraPerspectiveChanged Event dispatcher with the new perspective. First-person rendering listens to it.
    IsPerspectiveAvailable(Perspective) True when the perspective is allowed (and, for first person, the pawn has AC_FirstPersonRendering).
    GetNextAvailablePerspective, GetFirstAvailablePerspective What 1 would switch to; the fallback start view.
    GetCrosshairAim The aim ray and point. Only valid for player-controlled pawns.
    Get_AimRay (pawn interface) The same ray plus the trace channel and distance; zeros when the pawn has no camera system or no player controller.
    CameraPerspective, CameraStyle, bIsCameraRightShoulder Current state.

    Networking¶

    The perspective is set on the owning client and sent to the server (Server_SetCameraPerspective); it replicates to other machines. Style, shoulder and crosshair are local to the player.

    Limitations¶

    • A new style or mode needs matching chooser rows and rigs.
    • The offset rigs (crouch, shoulder, cover) apply in third person only.
    • The main rig chooser table can't be swapped per pawn (see How the rig is chosen).
    • Rig graph edits need the Gameplay Cameras editor; save each rig asset after editing.
