    Release notes¶

    Unreleased¶

    Added¶

    • First-person motion: procedural hand and item motion in first person (look sway, move sway, bob, breathing, jump and landing kicks), fading while aiming down sights; the camera never moves. Tuned through motion profiles (PDA_FirstPersonMotion; Unarmed, Pistol, Rifle, Heavy), per item (First Person Motion Profile on the equipment data) or per character, with a global First Person Motion Intensity and a disable_fp_motion montage curve. See Tune first-person motion.
    • Camera: Allowed Perspectives lists the views players can use: first person only, third person only, or any mix. The perspective input cycles them in order; the start view and the ragdoll view fall back when not allowed. It replaces Enable First Person Rendering: first-person meshes are only built when first person is allowed. Third Person Camera Bone sets the third-person attach bone; Show Crosshair hides the crosshair dot.
    • First person: First Person Camera Bone and First Person Head Bone per character, and the advanced First Person Pipeline Anim Class, First Person Retarget Host Anim Class and First Person Mesh Suffix.
    • Cover: Enable Cover Run, Enable Cover Shoulder Switch and Enable Cover Camera Offsets. The cover marker shows at the distance where the cover button takes sticky cover.
    • Equipment: Left Hand Bone / Right Hand Bone on the equipment component and Hand IK Socket / ADS Socket per item.
    • Overlays: Overlay Pose Layers and Overlay Body Layers maps: a new stance or body style is a map row, not a graph edit. A value with no row prints a warning.
    • Characters: Default Visual Override and Follow Global Visual Override per pawn, for fixed characters and for game modes other than GM_Sandbox.
    • Collision: a Cover trace channel decides what counts as cover; every profile answers it like Visibility, and objects can opt out (Ignore) or in (Block). The GASPALS channels moved to the last slots (ECC_GameTraceChannel15 to 18) so they don't clash with a project's own channels; copy the new collision settings.
    • Trace settings: Traversable Trace Channel and Room Check Trace Channel (traversal), Cover Trace Channel (cover), Aim Trace Channel and Aim Trace Distance (camera; aim from cover uses them too), and the bench interaction's range, box size and object types.
    • Mover pawn: Ragdoll On Obstacle Hit, Ragdoll Obstacle Object Types and Ragdoll Obstacle Trace Radius for the obstacle course.
    • Input: Input Mapping Contexts on the pawns (context and priority); swap or add contexts without editing the graph.
    • Widgets: Crosshair Widget Class (camera), Cover Marker Widget Class, Cover Marker Draw Size, Cover Marker Wall Offset and Cover Target Crosshair Color (cover), Equipment Menu Widget Class, Overlay Pose Menu Widget Class and Overlay Body Menu Widget Class, and Menu Time Dilation on the equipment and overlay components.
    • Camera offset rigs: Camera Offset Rigs on the camera component (crouch, right shoulder, low cover, cover edges, first-person scale): per pawn, and an empty entry turns that offset off.
    • Pawn interface: Get_AimRay returns the pawn's aim ray with its trace channel and distance.
    • CMC movement tuning: Gait Max Acceleration, Gait Ground Friction (walk, run, sprint, with sprint speed ranges), Braking Deceleration, Stopping Braking Friction and Falling Rotation Rate on the CMC pawn (they were fixed values in its graphs).
    • Auto-strafe and target lock on the CMC pawn, with the same settings as the Mover pawn; the CMC auto-strafe state replicates with the input state.
    • Sprint Max Angle From Facing on both pawns; Slide Enter Speed / Slide Exit Speed on the Slide mode; Just Landed Deceleration / Duration on the Walking mode; Falling Rotation Rate on the Falling mode.
    • Traversal check settings on AC_TraversalLogic (trace radius, half heights, speed and reach ranges, air reach), per pawn.
    • Hold or toggle per action on both pawns: Toggle Crouch / Walk / Sprint / Aim / Strafe (defaults unchanged).
    • Cover debug: Enable Debug on the cover component now draws the cover traces (it did nothing before; the Mover cover component had it on, now off).
    • Camera movement mode: the pawns now pass their movement mode to the camera (Pass Movement Mode To Camera, on), and new offset rigs pull the camera back in the air, during traversal and while ragdolling, and lower it while sliding (In Air / Sliding / Traversing / Ragdoll Rig in Camera Offset Rigs).
    • Feel: Ready Hold Time Idle / Moving and Aim Min Time per overlay; ADS Enter / Exit Speed and ADS Hand Offset per item; First Person Camera Vertical Smoothing, First Person Full Body View and First Person Max Root Yaw Lag on the first-person component; cover Side Input Threshold, Top Probe Height, Probe Padding, cover run timing and stand offset, Sticky Reentry Cooldown; first-person traversal turn speed and threshold.
    • Debug tools: the AC_DebugTools component on the Mover pawn (/GASPALS/Systems/DebugTools) holds the demo shot (aim + Left Button), spawn or rotate (aim + E), teleport (J) and debug flying (Z), with Enable Debug Tools (one switch to turn them all off), Shot Trace Distance, Shot Impulse, Spawn Trace Distance, Max Spawned Characters and Teleport Max Distance. The keys are unchanged. See Debug tools.
    • Ragdoll settle (Mover): Ragdoll Settle Speed, Ragdoll Settle Delay and Ragdoll Settle Blend Time: a ragdoll lying still fades its physics control strengths out.
    • CMC ragdoll entry: Inherited Velocity Scale, Left Leg Bones and Right Leg Bones on AC_RagdollSystem.

    Changed¶

    • One Mover pawn. SandboxCharacter_Mover_Ragdoll is merged into SandboxCharacter_Mover and removed. The Mover pawn now has the ragdoll movement mode (X, Space to get up), the Physics Control component and its profiles, takedowns and shoves (Left Button from a standstill near another Mover character, E), hit reactions and the injury states, in every game mode. GM_Sandbox_Ragdoll spawns SandboxCharacter_Mover, and the characters placed in DefaultLevel and RagdollLevel are Mover pawns. If your project uses SandboxCharacter_Mover_Ragdoll (a child Blueprint, a cast, a placed actor or a game mode setting), point it at SandboxCharacter_Mover.
    • Teleport (J) on the Mover pawn goes through its debug tools (their own Teleport Max Distance); other pawns keep the player controller's teleport.
    • Systems talk less to each other. The camera component only creates and places the crosshair; the crosshair widget only draws the dot. The cover system runs its own targeting and marker for the local player and reads the aim ray through the pawn interface. The menu components keep the highlighted entry and the menu widgets only display it. Custom crosshairs and menus no longer need specific functions (W_Crosshair.SetCoverTargeted and the switchers' CycleState are gone).
    • Mover pawn: X (IA_Ragdoll, gamepad face-left) ragdolls it, the same key as the CMC pawn; R (IA_TriggerRagdoll) is no longer mapped.

    Fixed¶

    • Overlays: a stance set on the pawn shows at spawn and comes back after unequipping (the Unequip slot no longer links the default pose over it).
    • Menus: the equipment and overlay menus open for the pawn's own player controller (not player 0). Destroying the pawn with only the body menu open now removes that menu too.
    • Multiplayer: the Mover pawn's possession setup and the CMC pawn's input state RPC are reliable.
    • Mover pawn: the ragdoll movement mode reads the pawn's capsule size instead of fixed values.
    • Camera: the Mover pawn no longer prints an AC_RagdollSystem warning (the ragdoll camera settings apply to the CMC pawn).
    • CMC ragdoll: leaving ragdoll restores the capsule and mesh collision the pawn had before, instead of fixed values (the mesh used to stay queryable as Pawn after the first ragdoll).
    • Mover pawn: ragdoll entry, exit and takedowns restore the capsule's own collision profile instead of a fixed CharacterCapsule.
    • CMC ragdoll: the get-up works again. Leaving ragdoll puts the mesh back on the capsule (the simulated pelvis had dragged it off, so the character stayed tilted on the ground and the get-up played lying down).
    • Overlays: aiming with an item works again. The overlay children (Rifle, Bow, Pistol 2H, Torch, Binoculars and the others) had Ready Hold Time Idle / Moving and Aim Min Time stored as 0, which dropped Ready back to Relaxed before Aiming could start; they now use the base values (3 / 2 / 0.75 s).
    • Cover: walls close to High Cover Height (1.25 m on the sample walls) no longer make the character crouch and stand every frame: the height is measured from the feet (High Cover Height is now 148 cm above the feet, the same standing probe as before).
    • Cover: a tap of the cover button prefers sticky cover and a hold prefers the cover run, also from sticky cover or right in front of other cover; a started cover run finishes after the button is released (press again to cancel).
    • Crosshair: the dot shows again on both pawns at all times (it was placed at the camera position instead of the aim point). It no longer turns green on cover targets: the cover marker shows them, and Cover Target Crosshair Color is gone.
    • Mover pawn: crouching while walking or running no longer slides, and slides end again (the pawn's slide mode had Slide Enter / Exit Speed stored as 0).
    • CMC pawns placed in a level (NPCs) no longer log Accessed None errors from cover at play start.
    • Menus: mouse wheel up and D-pad up move the highlight up the list again (they moved it down) in the equipment, pose and body menus.
    • Mover pawn: hands and arms no longer catch on walls (for example the left hand in low cover while aiming and turning, or the left hand off the rifle grip): outside ragdoll the body is kinematic by default (DDCVar.CharacterPhysics.DefaultProfile is now 0). Set it to 1 for the physical animation profile.
    • Mover pawn: vaulting and mantling from cover work again. One traversal call in the pawn ran twice per press, and the second start could cancel the first: from low cover the character was thrown high into the air, from high cover it stayed in place.
    • Mover ragdoll: the hands no longer shake on a ragdoll lying still. The arms chased an animated pose that kept moving; now the physics control strengths fade out once the body has settled (see Ragdoll Settle settings above) and come back when it moves.
    • CMC ragdoll: a running character keeps its momentum when it goes ragdoll instead of dropping in place, and its legs no longer pass through each other (the same leg collision as the Mover ragdoll).

    Removed¶

    • SandboxCharacter_Mover_Ragdoll (merged into SandboxCharacter_Mover, see Changed).
    • The ragdoll pawn's demo crosshair (Demo_SimpleCrosshair); the camera system's crosshair is the only one.

    1.0 fixes (October 2026)¶

    Fixed¶

    • Multiplayer: the Mover pawns' camera and traversal components and the CMC pawn's visual override manager now replicate, as on the other pawn. The camera perspective replicates both ways on the Mover pawns, and a client's character change reaches every machine on the CMC pawn.
    • Traversal no longer starts twice for a pawn the server controls locally (listen-server host, standalone play, server AI).
    • Equipment: a client sends its selection to the server once instead of twice. Unequipping resets the hand IK targets instead of keeping the last item's values. The item cleanup function is now spelled DestroySpawnedActors.
    • Menus: destroying a pawn while the equipment or overlay menu is open removes the menu and restores normal world speed (CloseEquipmentMenu, CloseOverlayMenus).
    • First person: visual override characters no longer get a duplicate first-person body mesh.
    • Top-down and side-scroller cameras (CMC): other machines' copies of a CMC pawn no longer log an error every frame while that pawn uses a fixed camera; only the locally controlled pawn sets its control rotation.
    • Overlays: Injured (one hand held to the side) and HandsTied (hands behind the back) are real overlays now. A broken reference to deleted source animations on their crouch poses was cleared.
    • Copy-pose characters: ABP_CopyPose_UEFN has Copy Curves on, so the hand IK curves reach characters on the UEFN skeleton.
    • Several variable and function descriptions that repeated their own text were fixed.

    Changed¶

    • Cover is on by default: Enable Cover is now true on the cover component class, and Auto Crouch Low Cover is on for every sample pawn. The Mover ragdoll pawn uses the same cover facing as the Mover pawn.
    • The Mover debug flying toggle moved from the gamepad left stick click to Z (keyboard only); the left stick click only toggles strafe.
    • Overlay changes are instant: the unused transition animation nodes were removed.
    • Kellan's Blueprint is renamed BP_RetargetedCharacter_Kellan.
    • CameraShake_Basic moved to /GASPALS/Systems/CameraSystem.

    Removed¶

    • The old camera copy /GASPALS/Blueprints/Cameras, ABP_Equipment_HandIK, the unused IMC_GASPALS_* mapping contexts, the ... (2) duplicate input assets and IA_Traverse (traversal runs from IA_Jump).
    • CameraRig_FP_Offset_Crouch: the first-person camera follows the head, which already lowers it when crouching.

    Tested after these fixes: both pawns in single player, a listen server with a client on both pawns (traversal, camera perspective, character changes), the retargeted characters with a rifle in third and first person, and a packaged Win64 build.

    1.0 (October 2026)¶

    First public version for Unreal Engine 5.8, built on the Game Animation Sample (5.8).

    Foundation¶

    • All content moved into the GASPALS plugin (/GASPALS); the plugin declares the engine plugins it needs.
    • Two pawns at feature parity: Mover with Network Prediction (plus a ragdoll variant) and the Character Movement Component.
    • Runtime-retargeted characters: Manny, Quinn and the MetaHuman Kellan.

    Systems¶

    • Overlay layering in the ALS style: overlay poses and body styles linked as animation layers, blended per body part by curves, with aim sweeps; the legs layer is enabled.
    • Equipment: data-driven items (rifle, pistol, bow, torch, binoculars, box, barrel) with a selection menu, hand IK and first-person copies generated at runtime.
    • Camera: Gameplay Cameras with a Blueprint camera director; third person (three distances), first person, top-down and side-scroller; crouch, shoulder and cover offsets; crosshair and aim ray.
    • First person: full-body first person generated at runtime, three modes, aim down sights, per-character eye offsets, MetaHuman clothing support.
    • Cover: soft and sticky cover, cover run along the navmesh, chained corner turns, vault from cover, aim from cover with the least exposed pose, pelvis lowering adapted to the cover height, per-case wall distances, cover marker, first-person behaviors. Multiplayer on both pawns.
    • Ragdoll and interactions: replicated Mover ragdoll with physical animation, get-ups, takedowns and shoves; CMC ragdoll component.
    • Movement: slide with a cooldown against slide spam.

    Quality¶

    • Snappier third-person weapon aim for the local player (aim lag 7.6 to 1.4 degrees).
    • Mover foot placement measures leg bone lengths, fixing bent knees under overlay legs.
    • Generated first-person copies have no collision.
    • Every system Blueprint has categories, descriptions and commented, formatted graphs.
    • The Win64 package builds.
    • Full documentation: getting started, concepts, a page per system, tutorials and a complete Blueprint reference.
